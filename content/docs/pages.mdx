## Implementation status

This destination is part of the working site scaffold. No installable templates are available yet. The approved design includes form layouts, application shells and other compositions; these will be built from the canonical components after their contracts are validated.

## Reuse requirements

Keep business rules in the owning application. Promote a composition into a shared pattern only after at least two applications demonstrate the same need.
