# Digital Brand Language

## Source

This direction is derived from the supplied TeacherActive 2025 brand-guideline screenshots. Exact production values must be confirmed from source-quality brand files before token implementation.

## Visual character

TeacherActive interfaces should feel confident, direct, energetic, supportive, and human. The system combines strong blue information structure with decisive orange emphasis and generous white space.

## Colour roles

- TeacherActive blue anchors navigation, headings, high-emphasis surfaces, and trusted information.
- Orange identifies primary actions, emphasis, selected brand moments, and energetic illustration details.
- Light blue supports secondary surfaces and information grouping.
- Teal, yellow, and status colours communicate supporting meaning through semantic tokens.
- True white remains the default page and reading surface.

Colour combinations must follow approved pairings. Blue and orange should not be blended into muddy gradients or used with insufficient contrast.

## Typography

- Use the approved brand families and weights when licensed font files are available.
- Use bold, compact headings with clear level changes.
- Orange subheadings may reinforce hierarchy, while body content remains quieter and highly readable.
- Product UI uses a restrained type scale; campaign-scale typography is reserved for marketing or brand moments.
- Controls, tables, charts, and navigation receive deliberate UI typography rather than browser defaults.

## Shape and composition

- Curves, waves, and the TeacherActive triangle create recognisable framing and directional energy.
- Standard controls remain geometrically simple with restrained radii and clear borders.
- Use branded shapes selectively in headers, banners, empty states, feature panels, and media framing.
- Avoid applying decorative curves to dense tables, forms, or every card.

## Icons and illustration

- Functional icons use consistent geometry, optical size, stroke/fill treatment, and blue/orange roles.
- Orange is normally the primary icon colour, with blue and light blue used for added detail where appropriate.
- Illustrations may use more brand detail than utility icons but must remain legible at their intended size.
- Emojis are for informal internal or social contexts and are not substitutes for interface icons.

## Photography

Use bright, candid imagery of education, recruitment, collaboration, and diverse people. Prefer authentic British school and workplace contexts. Avoid flat, overly staged, dark, or visually generic stock imagery.

## Product interpretation

The design system should achieve HeroUI-level polish through complete states, careful spacing, responsive behaviour, and theme ergonomics while retaining a recognisably TeacherActive visual language. Avoid generic purple gradients, glass surfaces, excessive shadows, and repetitive floating cards.
